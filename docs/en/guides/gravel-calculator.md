# Gravel calculator



Estimate gravel or sand by volume, weight, bags and cost.





## Get started



1. Choose a known area, rectangle or circle and enter the depth.
2. Choose the material. Use More options to enter a custom density or bag capacity.







## Example



### Input



```
200 ft² at 3 in, gravel at 1,680 kg/m³.
```



### Expected result



```
50 ft³ (1.42 m³), about 2,379 kg.
```







## Try it



[Open full tool](<https://toolpony.com/en/tools/gravel-calculator/>)







## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



Multiply area by depth, then apply the allowance. Mass equals volume times bulk density. For bag prices, round up to whole bags before multiplying by price. Use the supplier’s bulk density when available.



$$
V=Ad(1+w/100),\qquad m=\rho V,\qquad N=\left\lceil\frac{m}{m_{\rm bag}}\right\rceil\ \text{or}\ \left\lceil\frac{V}{V_{\rm bag}}\right\rceil
$$
