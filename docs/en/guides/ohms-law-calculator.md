# Ohm’s law calculator



Find voltage, current, resistance and power from any two quantities.





## Get started



1. Enter exactly two positive quantities and choose their units. Clear any other numeric fields.







## Example



### Input



```
12 V and 6 Ω
```



### Expected result



```
2 A, 24 W
```







## Try it



[Open full tool](<https://toolpony.com/en/tools/ohms-law-calculator/>)







## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



For a resistive circuit, V = IR and P = VI. Inputs convert to SI units first. This calculator uses 746 W per hp and displays results in V, A, Ω and W.



$$
V=IR,\qquad P=IV
$$
