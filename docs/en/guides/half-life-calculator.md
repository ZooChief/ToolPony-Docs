## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



Exponential decay is N(t) = N₀ · 2^(−t/T). T is half-life, mean lifetime is T/ln(2), and the decay constant is ln(2)/T. The conversion uses ln(2) rounded to ten decimal places. Use the same time unit for all fields.



$$
N(t)=N_0\,2^{-t/T},\qquad \lambda=\frac{\ln 2}{T},\qquad \tau=\frac1\lambda
$$



Pick the unknown first. Use consistent time units across the other three fields. For an initial amount of 100 and a half-life of 10, the remaining amount after 20 time units is 25.
