# Time duration calculator



Find intervals, add or subtract durations, and shift a date and time.





## Get started



1. Choose clocks for one shift, dates for a multi-day interval, or the expression mode for arithmetic.







## Example



### Input



```
22:30 to 06:15, overnight
```



### Expected result



```
07:45:00
```







## Try it



[Open full tool](<https://toolpony.com/en/tools/time-duration-calculator/>)







## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



A clock interval can cross midnight. With explicit dates, a day is 24 hours. For an expression, d, h, m and s mean days, hours, minutes and seconds. A minus applies to its whole group of time terms.



$$
T=86400d+3600h+60m+s
$$
