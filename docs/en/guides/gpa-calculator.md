## Calculation method



GPA is total grade points times credits divided by total credits. Letter grades use the displayed 4.3-point scale. Percentage mode averages percentages directly. Use explicit points for other school scales.



$$
\operatorname{GPA}=\frac{\sum_i g_i c_i}{\sum_i c_i}
$$



Enter one course per row. The fourth column can group courses by term. The fifth adds an explicit per-credit bonus for a course. Enter prior GPA and credits together when adding an existing record.



A+ through F use the 4.3-point mapping. P, NP, I, and W are omitted from the weighted sum. Use points mode to follow a school-specific numeric scale.
