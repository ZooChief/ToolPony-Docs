# Unit converter guide



Convert length, mass, area, volume, temperature, speed, energy, data and other units.





## Example



### Input



```
1 m to ft
```



### Expected result



```
≈3.28084 ft
```

## Check a temperature change



1. A reading falls from 20 °C to 10 °C. Choose Temperature difference and enter −10 °C. The change is −18 °F. A temperature reading of 10 °C is 50 °F.





## A 1 TB drive in GiB



A drive marked 1 TB contains 1,000,000,000,000 bytes. Dividing by 1,073,741,824 bytes per GiB gives about 931.32 GiB before formatting. The exact reading and CSV retain the final digits.



[Run example](<https://toolpony.com/en/tools/unit-converter/#example-drive-capacity>)







## A 3/8-inch fitting



Enter 3/8 inch to read 9.525 mm. Mixed numbers work too. A length of 1 3/8 in equals 34.925 mm.



[Run example](<https://toolpony.com/en/tools/unit-converter/#example-fractional-inch>)

## Calculation method



Defined unit ratios use exact fractions. A recurring decimal retains its fraction alongside the rounded reading. Conversions between radians and other angle units use an approximation of π.
