# Date calculator



Count calendar days and business days, or shift a date by calendar units or workdays.





## Get started



1. For a count, enter two dates and choose whether to include the later date. Enter holidays in More options.
2. To move a date earlier, enter a negative number. Use business-day shift to skip Saturdays, Sundays and your holidays.







## Example



### Input



```
2020-01-01 to 2020-02-01
```



### Expected result



```
31 calendar days, 23 business days
```







## Try it



[Open full tool](<https://toolpony.com/en/tools/date-calculator/>)







## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



Business days are Monday through Friday excluding your dates. Use YYYY-MM-DD once or MM-DD every year. A business-day shift counts the earlier endpoint and excludes the later one; the result can land on a weekend or holiday. Month shifts clamp to month-end.







## Calculation mode



**Between dates**

- Start date
- End date
- Count the end date
- Holidays (YYYY-MM-DD or MM-DD)





**Add or subtract dates**

- Start date
- Years
- Months
- Weeks
- Days





**Shift by business days**

- Start date
- Business days
- Holidays (YYYY-MM-DD or MM-DD)
