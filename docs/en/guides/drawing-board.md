# Drawing board



Draw on the canvas with a brush or eraser. Set the color and size, undo or redo strokes, then download a transparent PNG.





## Get started



1. Choose the brush or eraser and set its size.
2. Draw on the canvas with a mouse, finger or pen.
3. Undo or redo a stroke and export a transparent PNG.







## Try it



[Open full tool](<https://toolpony.com/en/tools/drawing-board/>)







## Good to know



Clearing the canvas can be undone within the available history. Export the PNG before closing the tab.







## Limits



The canvas is 1080 × 640 pixels. The drawing stays in this tab. Undo and redo use Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z.

[Documentation](https://docs.toolpony.com/en/guides/drawing-board/)
