## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



Scientific notation uses one leading digit. Engineering notation uses an exponent divisible by three. Addition, subtraction and multiplication use exact decimal coefficients before rounding to the selected precision.



$$
x=a\times10^n,\qquad 1\le |a|<10\quad(x\ne0)
$$



Enter 1568938, 1.568938e6, or 1.568938 × 10^6. They represent the same value. Engineering notation uses a power of ten divisible by three.



The arithmetic mode keeps exact decimal coefficients for addition, subtraction, and multiplication. Non-integer powers and square roots use floating-point approximation.
