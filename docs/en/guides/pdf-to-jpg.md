# PDF to JPG or PNG



Choose a PDF, select the pages you want and set the resolution. Download one image or a ZIP in page selection order.





## Get started



1. Choose an unlocked PDF.
2. Select thumbnails or enter page numbers. Choose JPG or PNG and a resolution.
3. Convert and download a single image or a ZIP in selection order.







## Example



### Input



```
One 612 × 792 pt page, PNG, 144 DPI
```



### Expected result



```
A 1224 × 1584 pixel PNG
```







## Try it



[Open full tool](<https://toolpony.com/en/tools/pdf-to-jpg/>)







## Good to know



PDF dimensions use 72 points per inch. At 144 DPI each point spans two pixels. Rotated pages keep their orientation.







## Limits



PDF up to 30 MB and 300 pages. Each export renders up to 40 pages and 60 megapixels total, with at most 8,192 pixels per side and 24 megapixels per page. ZIP images are limited to 64 MB. Pages render on white. Password-protected PDFs are rejected.
