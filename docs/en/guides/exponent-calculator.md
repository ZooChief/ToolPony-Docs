## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



For y = bⁿ, solve the exponent with logarithms when b and y are positive and b ≠ 1. Solving for a real base with a nonzero even integer exponent can produce two roots.



$$
y=b^p,\qquad p=\frac{\ln y}{\ln b},\qquad b=\sqrt[p]{y}
$$
