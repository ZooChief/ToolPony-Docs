# Macronutrient calculator guide



Convert a calorie target and nutrient ratios into grams, or calculate energy and proportions from grams.





## Example



### Input



```
2,000 kcal: 25% protein, 45% carbohydrate, 30% fat
```



### Expected result



```
125 g protein, 225 g carbohydrate, 66.67 g fat
```

## Divide a recipe into three servings



1. Enter 30 g protein, 60 g carbohydrate and 10 g fat for the whole recipe. The general 4/4/9 factors give 450 kcal. Three equal portions each contain 150 kcal, 10 g protein, 20 g carbohydrate and about 3.33 g fat.
2. For a packaged product, its label may use specific energy factors for fibre or sugar alcohols. Those factors can change the energy calculated from the same gram totals.

## Calculation method



General food-energy factors are 4 kcal/g for protein and carbohydrate and 9 kcal/g for fat. One blank percentage takes the remainder to 100%. Equal servings divide each nutrient and the energy by the same count.
