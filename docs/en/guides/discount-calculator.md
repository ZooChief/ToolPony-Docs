# Discount calculator



Stack discounts, subtract a fixed coupon and apply sales tax to the discounted price.





## Get started



1. Use the example values or enter your own measurements.
2. Calculate, then copy the result or download the available details.







## Example



### Input



```
100 with 20% and 10% off
```



### Expected result



```
72; combined discount 28%
```







## Try it



[Open full tool](<https://toolpony.com/en/tools/discount-calculator/>)







## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



Apply percentage discounts in order, then the fixed coupon, then tax. Up to 10 rates from 0% to 100%.



Two successive discounts of 20% and 10% leave 0.8 × 0.9 of the original price. Enter both rates in order, then add a coupon or tax if applicable.



Use an inverse mode when you know the sale price or the amount saved. Inverse modes handle one discount before tax.







## Calculation mode



**Apply discounts**

- Original price
- Discount percentages
- Fixed coupon
- Tax rate · %





**Find original price**

- Price after discount
- Discount type · Percentage / Fixed amount
- Discount





**Find rate and savings**

- Original price
- Known amount · Discounted price / Savings
- Amount





**Find prices from savings**

- Amount saved
- Discount rate · %
