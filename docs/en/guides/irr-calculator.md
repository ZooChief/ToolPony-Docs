# Internal rate of return calculator



Find the annual effective rate from irregular annual flows or a terminal value and regular deposits or withdrawals.





## Get started



1. Use the example values or enter your own measurements.
2. Calculate, then copy the result or download the available details.







## Example



### Input



```
55,000 outlay with 14,000, 18,000, 22,000, 26,000 inflows
```



### Expected result



```
IRR ≈ 15.093%
```

## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



IRR is a rate that makes net present value zero. Fixed-mode cash flows use the selected payment frequency and beginning or ending timing. Choose whether a beginning-of-period payment is also counted at the terminal boundary. The rate search covers −99.9999% to 10,000%.



$$
0=\sum_{i=0}^n\frac{F_i}{(1+r)^{t_i}}
$$







## Calculation mode



**Irregular annual flows**

- Initial investment
- Subsequent annual cash flows





**Fixed flows and ending balance**

- Initial investment
- Whole years
- Additional months
- Ending balance
- Payment amount
- Payment type · Withdrawal / Deposit
- Payments per year · 1 / 2 / 4 / 12 / 24 / 26 / 52
- Payment timing · End of period / Beginning of period
- Include the terminal payment in beginning mode
