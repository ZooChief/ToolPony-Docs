Calculate simple interest or solve for principal, interest rate, or term from the other inputs.

2. Calculate, then copy the result or download the available details.

## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



Interest equals principal × periodic rate × number of periods. Convert the term to the same unit as the rate before multiplying. The end balance adds interest to principal.



$$
I=Prt,\qquad A=P+I
$$



Match the term unit to the quoted rate period. For example, 5% per year applied for 24 months uses 2 annual periods.



Use the inverse modes when the end balance is known. A zero rate or zero term can make the inverse problem non-unique.
