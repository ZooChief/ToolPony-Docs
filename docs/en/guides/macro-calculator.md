# Macronutrient calculator



Convert a calorie target and nutrient ratios into grams, or calculate energy and proportions from grams.





## Get started



1. Use the example values or enter your own measurements.
2. Calculate, then copy the result or download the available details.







## Example



### Input



```
2,000 kcal: 25% protein, 45% carbohydrate, 30% fat
```



### Expected result



```
125 g protein, 225 g carbohydrate, 66.67 g fat
```







## Try it



[Open full tool](<https://toolpony.com/en/tools/macro-calculator/>)







## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



This calculation uses 4 kcal/g for protein and carbohydrate and 9 kcal/g for fat. Percentages in target mode must sum to 100.







## Calculation mode



**Target to grams**

- Total energy · kcal
- Protein · %
- Carbohydrate · %
- Fat · %





**Grams to energy**

- Protein · g
- Carbohydrate · g
- Fat · g
