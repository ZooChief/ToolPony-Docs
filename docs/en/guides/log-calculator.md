## Calculation method



The logarithm log₍b₎(x) is the exponent y satisfying bʸ = x. The change-of-base formula divides ln(x) by ln(b).



$$
\log_b(x)=\frac{\ln x}{\ln b},\qquad b>0,\quad b\ne1,\quad x>0
$$
