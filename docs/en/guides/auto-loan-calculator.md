# Auto loan calculator



Find a car payment or maximum vehicle price with a trade-in, outstanding debt, rebates and taxes.





## Get started



1. Enter the price or switch to a target monthly payment. Add the down payment, net trade-in inputs and any rebates.
2. Enter your tax and fees. Choose what lowers the tax base and whether to finance the charges, then export the schedule.







## Example



### Input



```
30,000 price, 5,000 down, 5% for 60 months
```



### Expected result



```
471.78 monthly, before tax and fees
```

The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



Enter the tax rate and choose which credits reduce the tax base. If financed, tax and fees are added to principal. A trade-in’s equity is its value minus debt. Results use fixed monthly interest.



$$
P=V-D-(T-O)-R+F,\quad M=\frac{Pr}{1-(1+r)^{-n}},\quad M=\frac Pn\; (r=0)
$$



V is vehicle price, D down payment, T trade-in value, O outstanding trade-in debt, R rebate, and F financed tax and fees. P is the financed principal. M is the monthly payment, r the monthly rate, and n the number of months.
