# Probability calculator



Calculate event probabilities, recover independent probabilities from two known values, or use a normal interval.





## Get started



1. Use the example values or enter your own measurements.
2. Calculate, then copy the result or download the available details.

## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



Independent events satisfy P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B). For dependent events, enter the intersection directly. Repeated trials multiply their probabilities, and the probability of at least one success uses the complement.



$$
P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)
$$



$$
P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)\quad\text{(independent)}
$$







## Calculation mode



**Two independent events**

- Probability of A
- Probability of B





**Known intersection**

- Probability of A
- Probability of B
- Both events





**Recover independent events**

- First known quantity · Probability of A / Probability of B / Not A / Not B / Both events / At least one event / Exactly one event / Neither event

- Second known quantity · Probability of A / Probability of B / Not A / Not B / Both events / At least one event / Exactly one event / Neither event
- Second probability





**Repeated independent trials**

- Probability of A
- Trials of A
- Probability of B
- Trials of B

- Upper bound
