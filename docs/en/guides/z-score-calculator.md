## Calculation method



The standard score is (x − μ)/σ. The left tail is the normal CDF at z. For symmetric regions the inverse returns the nonnegative boundary.



$$
z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma},\qquad P(a<Z<b)=\Phi(b)-\Phi(a)
$$
