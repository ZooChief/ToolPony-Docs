# BMR and calorie calculator



Estimate resting energy and daily energy expenditure in metric or US units with three equations.





## Get started



1. Use the example values or enter your own measurements.
2. Calculate, then copy the result or download the available details.







## Example



### Input



```
Male equation, 30 years, 70 kg, 175 cm, Mifflin–St Jeor
```



### Expected result



```
1,648.75 kcal/day at rest
```







## Try it



[Open full tool](<https://toolpony.com/en/tools/bmr-calculator/>)







## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



Resting energy is estimated with Mifflin–St Jeor, revised Harris–Benedict or Katch–McArdle. Daily expenditure multiplies the estimate by your activity factor.







## Calculation mode



**Metric**

- Age
- Equation group · Male / Female
- Weight · kg
- Height · cm
- Equation · Mifflin–St Jeor / Revised Harris–Benedict / Katch–McArdle
- Body fat for Katch–McArdle · %
- Activity factor
- Energy unit · kcal/day / kJ/day





**US customary**

- Age
- Equation group · Male / Female
- Weight · lb
- Height in feet · ft
- Additional inches · in
- Equation · Mifflin–St Jeor / Revised Harris–Benedict / Katch–McArdle
- Body fat for Katch–McArdle · %
- Activity factor
- Energy unit · kcal/day / kJ/day
