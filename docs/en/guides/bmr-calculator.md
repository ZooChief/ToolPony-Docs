# BMR and calorie calculator guide



Estimate resting energy and daily energy expenditure in metric or US units with three equations.





## Example



### Input



```
Male equation, 30 years, 70 kg, 175 cm, Mifflin–St Jeor
```



### Expected result



```
1,648.75 kcal/day at rest
```





<a id="case-study"></a>



## Estimate from lean mass



1. With a weight of 70 kg and an assumed body fat of 20%, lean mass is 56 kg. Katch–McArdle gives 370 + 21.6 × 56 = 1,579.6 kcal/day at rest.
2. A factor of 1.20 gives a daily estimate of 1,895.52 kcal. Changing that assumption to 1.55 gives 2,448.38 kcal. Both use the same resting estimate.

## Calculation method



Resting energy is estimated with Mifflin–St Jeor, revised Harris–Benedict or Katch–McArdle. Daily expenditure multiplies the estimate by your activity factor.







## Calculation mode





### Metric



- Equation · Mifflin–St Jeor / Revised Harris–Benedict / Katch–McArdle
- Age
- Equation group · Male / Female
- Weight · kg
- Height · cm
- Body fat for Katch–McArdle · %
- Activity factor
- Energy unit · kcal/day / kJ/day







### US customary



- Equation · Mifflin–St Jeor / Revised Harris–Benedict / Katch–McArdle
- Age
- Equation group · Male / Female
- Weight · lb
- Height in feet · ft
- Additional inches · in
- Body fat for Katch–McArdle · %
- Activity factor
- Energy unit · kcal/day / kJ/day
