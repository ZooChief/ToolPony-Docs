## Good to know



The calculation runs in your browser. Use consistent units in fields with no explicit unit.







## Calculation method



Monthly interest equals remaining principal times the annual rate divided by 12. Costs follow your annual inputs and growth rates. PMI can stop at 80% of the original home value in this model. Biweekly compares half of the scheduled monthly payment every two weeks with interest divided by 26.



$$
I_k=r B_{k-1},\qquad B_k=B_{k-1}-(M_k-I_k)
$$
